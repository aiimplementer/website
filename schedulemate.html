What it does

One shared calendar behind both your WhatsApp number and your public booking page.

📅

One shared calendar

Working hours, holidays, and one-off blocks all feed the same slot list — WhatsApp and the web page never disagree.

🚫

No overlaps, guaranteed

Double-bookings are rejected at the database, not just the form — even if two customers tap the same slot at once.

💬

Book by chat or by link

Share a public booking page, a WhatsApp number, or both — every booking lands in the same place either way.

🗂️

A desk view for staff

Confirm, check in, and close out appointments from a plain list sorted by time — built for a busy front desk, not a spreadsheet.

👥

Role-based staff access

Admins manage services and hours; staff run the daily desk; viewers get read-only access. Everyone sees only what they need.

🧾

Customers, built in

Every phone number that books becomes a customer record automatically — visit history included, no separate CRM to maintain.

How it works

From a customer's message to a closed-out appointment, in four steps.

1

Customer picks a time

Via your WhatsApp number or your public booking link — whichever they reach for first.

2

Slot is locked in

It's checked against working hours, holidays, and existing bookings before it's confirmed.

3

Your desk sees it instantly

The appointment appears on the shared list and calendar the moment it's booked.

4

Staff take it from there

Confirm, check the customer in, and mark it complete — or reschedule and cancel when plans change.
