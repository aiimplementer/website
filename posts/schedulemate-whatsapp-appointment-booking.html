ScheduleMate: WhatsApp Appointment Booking Without the Double-Bookings

One shared calendar behind your WhatsApp number and your booking page — so no two customers ever land on the same slot.

Most small businesses juggle appointments across WhatsApp chats, phone calls, and a booking page that never quite agrees with either. ScheduleMate puts all three behind one calendar, so a slot booked anywhere is blocked everywhere.

1. Why booking over chat alone breaks down

A customer messages on WhatsApp asking for a slot, you reply an hour later, and by then someone else has already taken it on the phone. Multiply that across a busy week and you get double-bookings, awkward calls to reschedule, and customers who simply go to the next business that replies faster.

2. One shared calendar behind WhatsApp and your booking page

ScheduleMate gives your business a public booking link and a WhatsApp number that both read from the same calendar. Working hours, holidays, and one-off blocks all feed the same slot list, so whichever way a customer books, your desk sees one consistent schedule.

3. No overlaps, even when two people book at once

Double-bookings are rejected at the database level, not just on the form — so even if two customers tap the same slot within seconds of each other, only one booking goes through. The other sees an updated list of open times instead of a conflict you have to sort out later.

4. A simple desk view for staff, not a spreadsheet

Every booking lands on a plain list sorted by time, where staff can confirm, check customers in, and close out appointments without touching a spreadsheet. Role-based access means admins manage services and hours, staff run the daily desk, and viewers get read-only access — everyone sees only what they need.

5. Rules that adapt to your business, not the other way round

Give each service its own hours and slot length, close a single day or block out an hour without touching your regular schedule, and set recurring breaks like a daily lunch closure that repeat on their own. Every phone number that books also becomes a customer record automatically, with visit history included — no separate CRM to maintain.
